# Start here — Sabine Research Desk 95

## The one-click route

After the Pages workflow has deployed, open:

**https://qsolkcb.github.io/sabine-gpt/**

That is the normal interface. No cloning, terminal, Python, JSON editing, GitHub knowledge, or account is required.

## What the buttons do

- **Work** — public, source-attributed research themes in the current snapshot.
- **Papers** — selected paper records with exact arXiv revisions and DOI metadata.
- **Atlas** — a visual navigation map. Its lines mean registry membership only, not theoretical equivalence.
- **Brief** — build a small research packet by choosing the question and relevant papers.
- **Sources** — inspect the public evidence behind the snapshot.
- **Help** — scope, independence, copyright, privacy, and AI-access boundaries.

Favourites, reviewed-paper marks, and brief selections stay in the current browser. The interface has no account, telemetry, analytics, advertising, or remote application server.

## Important boundary

The deployed site asks automated systems not to crawl, index, train on, embed, summarise, or ingest it. A human may deliberately export a small packet from the **Brief** screen.

GitHub Pages is public. The policy, page metadata, `robots.txt`, and machine-readable access declaration deter compliant systems but do not provide authentication, encryption, or secrecy. Confidential information must never be placed in the Pages interface or this public repository.
